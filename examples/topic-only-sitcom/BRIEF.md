# One key for every lock — production brief

> **How this started.** The whole request was one line:
> `/script-to-video:make-video a 35-second sitcom-style video for small business owners about password managers`
> The skill asked: *who watches?* (bakery and shop owners) · *angle?* (sitcom gag: busy mum, calm kid, a cutaway) ·
> *who is on screen?* (a cast of three) · *who speaks?* (dialogue) · *where?* (one back office + one cutaway) ·
> *call to action?* ("Comment KEYS") · *still or animated?* (animated) · *pictures?* (made here) · *music?* (new) ·
> *text?* (bold yellow TV subtitles). Everything below was written by the skill from those answers.

**Format:** vertical 9:16, 35 s, 5 scenes · **Motion:** animated (free acting clips, last scene loops)
**Pictures:** made here · **Slug:** one-key-for-every-lock
**Audience:** small business owners who reuse one password everywhere; after watching they should set up a password manager.
**Angle:** sitcom gag — a busy bakery owner buried in sticky-note passwords, her calm son explains, a key-vault cutaway shows the idea.
**Call to action:** "Comment KEYS and we'll send the beginner's checklist."

## Style lock
Flat cel-shaded TV sitcom animation, rounded soft characters with oversized heads, big oval eyes with small dot pupils,
thick even black outlines, flat colours with almost no shading, clean flat pastel backgrounds, simple furniture,
even sitcom lighting, wide level camera, original characters only. No text in any picture.
