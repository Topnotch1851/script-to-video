# Example: the request

The whole request was one line plus the script:

```text
/script-to-video:make-video a 30-second vertical explainer, still pictures I'll make myself, subtitles, new light music:

Ever wondered why the sky is blue and not green or pink?
Sunlight looks white, but it is really every colour mixed together.
When it hits the air, tiny bits of air bounce the colours around.
Blue light is bounced the most, so blue reaches your eyes from every direction.
At sunset, light travels through much more air, so the reds and oranges win.
Now you know. Look up, and tell someone why!
```

The skill asked only what was still open (style, voice, platforms), arranged the script into its built-in architect,
showed the cost for each model level, and produced `shotlist.json` and `prompts/PICTURE-PROMPTS.md` (the exact prompts
for the six pictures). Quote: about $0.12 (voice + one music track); pictures made by you cost nothing here.
