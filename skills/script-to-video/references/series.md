# Running a series

A series keeps one **series bible** (`templates/SERIES-BIBLE-template.md`), saved where it survives between chats
(the person's project files, a repo, or their drive). Read it before planning any new episode; update it after delivery.

## Locked for the whole series
Character sheet URL and description · voice name and settings · style lock · text style and colours · layout ·
naming pattern (`<series>-epNN-<topic>`) · target length and shape · price band.

## Changed every episode (variety keeps viewers)
Outfit colour (keep a list of colours used) · settings (every beat a new place) · music (new track, new mood or instrument)
· hook line · topic.

## Continuity rules
- **Never repeat a line** from an earlier episode. Keep a "lines used" list in the bible and check new scripts against it.
- **Open with a callback** to the previous episode ("Last time, magic e made a say its name. Today…").
- **End with a teaser** for the next episode, and put the same teaser in the pinned comment.
- Note anything weak in the last episode and fix it in this one.

## After each episode
Add to the bible: episode number, topic, date, cost, outfit colour, music mood, settings used, every narration line,
and what to fix next time.
