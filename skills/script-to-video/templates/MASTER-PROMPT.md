# Master prompt — Script to Video

**You do not need this prompt.** With the skill installed, one line is enough:

```text
/script-to-video:make-video a 35-second sitcom-style video for business owners about what MCP is
```
(or, in the Claude app, just: "Use script-to-video to make me a video about …"). The skill asks the questions, writes the
script and a full production brief (cast, places, every scene, voices, sound, cost) and waits for your "approved".

Use this prompt when you want to give everything at once — your own script and your answers — so there are fewer
questions. Copy everything inside the box into a new chat, paste your script (or a topic), fill in what you know and
leave the rest blank. Nothing that costs money happens until you type **approved**.

````text
MAKE MY VIDEO

Use the script-to-video skill. You are my producer; I am the creative director.
Follow the skill's workflow exactly: correct anything below that breaks its rules (and tell me what you changed),
ask me only the questions I left blank, show me the full cost and a spending cap before anything is paid for,
and stop at every approval gate until I say "approved".

=== MY SCRIPT ===
(paste your script here — or a topic, and we will write the script together first)



=== MY ANSWERS (leave blank to be asked) ===
Length:                 (e.g. 30 s / 60 s / 5 min — or "match the script")
Shape:                  (vertical / landscape / square — or "suggest from length")
Style:                  (e.g. storybook cartoon, stick figure, flat vector, 3D cartoon, watercolour,
                         cinematic real-life, my own footage — or "suggest")
Still or animated:      (still = free camera moves / animated / mixed)
Pictures:               (make them here / I'll make them myself — give me a prompt for each / my own photos or footage)
Angle & call to action: (e.g. "sitcom gag: clueless dad, smart daughter" · "Comment MCP for the guide")
On screen:              (nobody / one presenter: describe / a cast of 2–4: names + looks / a real person with consent)
Locations:              (one main room + cutaways: describe / a new place every scene / abstract)
Who speaks:             (unseen narrator / one character narrates / dialogue between characters / no voice)
Voice:                  (AI voice — name or "suggest" / my own recording) · language · pace
Music:                  (new music — mood / my own track / none)
On-screen text:         (none / subtitles / key words / teaching words with colour changes)
Audience & platforms:   (e.g. kids 4–7, Instagram + TikTok + YouTube + Facebook)
Budget limit:           (optional, e.g. "$3 max")
Series:                 (optional: series name + paste the series bible, or "one-off")

=== WHAT I EXPECT BACK ===
1. Quote and plan (free): beat table, style, text plan, cost, spending cap. Wait for "approved".
2. Look pilot: one picture (and one clip if animated) — or, if I make the pictures, a prompt for every picture,
   the layout guide, naming and upload steps. Wait for "approved".
3. Voice + all pictures + a free preview (no music yet). Wait for "approved".
4. Animation (if chosen) + music + final video, checked. Wait for "ship it".
5. Handoff: the video, captions file (.srt), thumbnail, SOCIAL.md with captions/hashtags/titles/tags for every
   platform I use, production log with every cost, and one zip with everything.
At every step: show the assets here, give me your honest one-line opinion of each, report money spent vs the quote.
````

## Tips
- **Cheapest possible:** "Pictures: I'll make them myself", "Voice: my own recording", "Music: none", "Still" → $0 paid.
- **Old prompt from somewhere else?** Paste it in the script box. It will be corrected to this standard first.
- **A series:** keep the series bible the skill gives you after episode 1 and paste it into "Series" every time.
